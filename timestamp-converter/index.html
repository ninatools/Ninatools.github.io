What is a Unix timestamp?

A Unix timestamp represents the number of seconds elapsed since January 1, 1970, at 00:00:00 UTC. Some systems use milliseconds instead of seconds.

Seconds vs. milliseconds

A timestamp in seconds commonly has 10 digits for current dates. A timestamp in milliseconds commonly has 13 digits. Choose automatic detection or select the unit yourself.

Does the time zone matter?

Unix timestamps represent a moment in UTC. The same timestamp may display as a different local date and time depending on your time zone.
