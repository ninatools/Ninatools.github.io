About NinaTools

Simple tools. Less hassle.

NinaTools is a collection of simple, fast and privacy-friendly online tools designed to make everyday digital tasks easier.

What is NinaTools?

NinaTools is built to provide useful online tools that are easy to understand and simple to use. Our goal is to help people complete everyday digital tasks without unnecessary steps.

We focus on practical tools for images, documents, QR codes, internet utilities and other common digital tasks.

Our approach

We believe online tools should be simple, clear and accessible. You should be able to open a tool, understand what it does, complete your task and move on.

Whenever possible, NinaTools aims to process files directly in your browser rather than requiring them to be uploaded to a server. The exact processing method may vary depending on the tool.

Privacy matters

We aim to minimize unnecessary collection of personal information and design our tools with privacy in mind.

For details about how information may be handled, please see our Privacy Policy.

Built to grow

NinaTools will continue to add useful tools based on what people actually need. We want to build a helpful collection of tools rather than a large number of pages that provide little value.
