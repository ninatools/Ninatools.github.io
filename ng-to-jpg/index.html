How to convert PNG to JPG

  1. Click Choose PNG Images and select your PNG files.
  2. Adjust the JPG quality if needed.
  3. Click Convert All or convert an individual image.
  4. Click Download JPG to save each converted image.

Why convert PNG to JPG?

JPG is widely supported and can produce smaller files for photographs. Unlike PNG, JPG does not support transparency, so transparent areas are filled with white.
