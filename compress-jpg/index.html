How to compress JPG files

  • Select one or more JPG/JPEG images.
  • Choose a quality level. Higher quality usually means a larger file.
  • Tap Compress JPG, compare the original and compressed sizes, then download the result.

Privacy

Your images are processed in your browser and are not uploaded to NinaTools. Compressed images are saved as JPEG files.
