About the exchange rates

Rates are provided by the free Frankfurter API and reflect published reference rates. They may update on business days rather than continuously and can differ from the rates, fees or spreads offered by banks, card providers and currency-exchange services. This tool is for informational estimates only.

Source: Frankfurter API.

How to use the currency converter

  1. Choose the currency you want to convert from and the currency you want to convert to.
  2. Enter the amount. Use a positive number and check that you selected the correct currency codes.
  3. Review the converted amount, the exchange rate and the date attached to the rate, when shown.
  4. Change the amount or currencies to compare another conversion.

Understanding a reference exchange rate

An exchange rate expresses how much of one currency corresponds to one unit of another. For example, a EUR-to-USD rate is used to estimate the US dollar value of an amount entered in euros. The result is a calculation based on the rate supplied to the tool; it is not a quote or an offer to exchange money.

Reference rates are useful for budgeting, travel planning and comparing approximate values. The amount you actually receive from a bank, money-transfer service, card provider or exchange counter may be different because providers can apply their own rates, fees, commissions and spreads.

When the displayed rate may differ

  • Update timing: reference rates may be published on business days and may not reflect market movements in real time.
  • Provider pricing: banks and payment services set their own customer rates and may add charges.
  • Rounding: the displayed result may be rounded for readability; a provider may calculate the final amount differently.
  • Availability: the converter depends on the external Frankfurter service. If rates cannot be retrieved, try again later rather than treating an old value as current.

Frequently asked questions

Is this a live trading or bank exchange rate?

No. The tool displays reference exchange-rate data from Frankfurter. It is intended for estimates and comparisons, not for executing a transaction.

Why might my bank show a different amount?

Your bank or payment provider may use a different rate, include a conversion margin, charge a fee, or update its rates at a different time.

Can I use the result for an international transfer?

Use it as an estimate only. Check the final rate, fees and amount directly with the provider before sending money.

What should I do if the rates do not load?

Check your internet connection and try again later. The rate service is external to NinaTools, so temporary service interruptions may affect availability.
