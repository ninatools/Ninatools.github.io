URL Encoder & Decoder

Encode special characters in URLs or decode URL-encoded text. Free, simple and processed directly in your browser.

How to use the URL Encoder & Decoder

Encode URL: Converts text into a URL-compatible format. Spaces become %20 and special characters are encoded.

Decode URL: Converts percent-encoded text back into readable text.

Copy Result: Copies the converted text to your clipboard.

Example

Original text:

hello world & tea

Encoded result:

hello%20world%20%26%20tea

Privacy

Your text is processed locally in your browser. This tool does not send your input to a NinaTools server.
