Split PDF Online for Free

Extract the pages you need from a PDF and save them as separate PDF files. Simple, fast, and free.

Your privacy matters. Your PDF is processed locally in your browser. This tool does not upload your document to NinaTools servers.
📄
Choose your PDF file

Select a PDF document from your device to get started.

Enter page numbers or ranges separated by commas. Each comma-separated group becomes a separate PDF. For example, 1-3, 5, 8-10 creates three files. Page numbers start at 1.

Download ZIP

How to split a PDF

  1. Select a PDF file from your device.
  2. Enter the pages you want, such as 1-3, 5, 8-10.
  3. Click Split PDF to create separate PDF files.
  4. Click Download ZIP to save all the extracted PDFs together.

Tip: Use 1-5 to create one PDF containing pages 1 through 5. Use 1-3, 4-6 to create two separate PDFs.
