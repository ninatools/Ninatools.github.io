Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters.

This Privacy Policy explains how NinaTools handles information when you use our website and online tools.

Information we collect

NinaTools is designed to minimize unnecessary collection of personal information.

When you use tools that process files directly in your browser, your files are not uploaded to our server by the tool itself.

How our tools process files and information

Several tools process selected images, PDFs, text or other input directly in your browser. For those tools, the input is not uploaded to NinaTools by the tool code. Some pages load the software libraries they need from third-party content delivery networks. Those providers may receive technical connection information when your browser requests the library. The page-specific notes explain relevant processing details.

A small number of tools need an external service to return information. The DNS Lookup tool sends the domain name you enter to Google's DNS-over-HTTPS resolver. The public IP checker requests your public IP address from the ipify service. The currency converter requests reference exchange-rate data from the Frankfurter API. These services receive the information needed to answer the request and process it under their own terms.

Hosting and website analytics

NinaTools is hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information, such as IP address, browser information and request logs, to deliver, maintain and protect the hosting service. See GitHub's General Privacy Statement for details.

NinaTools uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand aggregate page views and website performance. Cloudflare states that its Web Analytics does not use cookies or local storage for analytics and does not fingerprint visitors. Please consult Cloudflare Web Analytics documentation and the Cloudflare Privacy Policy for current details.

Cookies, advertising and similar technologies

The current NinaTools pages reviewed for this policy do not contain Google AdSense advertising code. If advertising or additional analytics services are introduced, this policy will be updated to identify the relevant services, explain their purposes and describe applicable consent choices before those services are activated where required.

Third-party websites and services linked from NinaTools have their own privacy policies and practices. NinaTools does not control how those external providers process information.

Contact and policy updates

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how a tool works, please use the Contact page. This policy may be updated when website features, service providers or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page.
